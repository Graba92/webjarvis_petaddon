# 🐾 Release: Desktop Pet & Tamagotchi AI Companion v2.5 (OpenPets Mini Core)

> **Bring a playful, health-guarding Tamagotchi companion to your Linux desktop!** A 100% standalone, anti-aliased PyQt6 desktop buddy featuring OpenPets V2 spritesheets, universal LLM API-key configuration (Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama), 4 distinct character voices, Tamagotchi vitals (hunger, thirst, breaks), fetch mini-game, deep sleep mode, Pomodoro timer, RPG progression, and a relentless nagging mode!
