# 🐾 Release: WebJarvis Pet Addon (OpenPets Mini Core)

> **Bring your AI assistant to life!** A lightweight, animated companion for WebJarvis featuring OpenPets V2 spritesheet support, 16-sector cursor gaze tracking, and real-time Jarvis telemetry reactions.
