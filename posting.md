# 🐾 Release: Yuyu's Cozy Apartment & The Traveling Merchant (Web Game Edition v1.0 / Suite v5.0)

> **Step into Yuyu's cozy isometric apartment and meet the Traveling Merchant!** A rich, interactive, standalone browser-based Tamagotchi game featuring 6 isometric rooms, animated Chibi Yuyu with pathfinding and micro-animations, the playful Kitty Companion, a deep dual-currency economy (Slow Coins 🪙 & Care Hearts ❤️), random room decoration gifts, dynamic weather, and the Traveling Merchant with his wooden cart and rotating shelf wares!
